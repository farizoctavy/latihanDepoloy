Simple Omelette Repice

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal, This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your chooice of cheese, vegetables, pr meats

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximatively 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, vegetables, ham, etc.

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: In a bowl, beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper until well mixed. They should be uniform in color and slightly frothy.
  2. Heat the pan: Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Warm the butter: Once the butter is melted, tilt the pan to coat it evenly.
  4. Pour in the eggs: Pour the beaten eggs into the pan, immediately reducing heat to low.
  5. Cook gently: Let the eggs cook slowly without stirring, until they begin to set around the edges.
  6. Fold and serve: When the eggs are almost set, gently fold them in half. Serve immediately on a plate.
